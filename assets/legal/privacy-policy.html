1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PRERNA INFO TECH SOLUTIONS (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects information when you use the mobile game Bloc Quest (the “Game”). By installing or playing the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: Bloc Quest does not require you to create an account or share your name, email or phone number to play. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Information collected automatically: When you use the Game, we and our third-party partners may automatically collect:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and approximate location derived from your IP address

Information stored on your device: Game progress, high scores and settings are stored locally on your device.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain and improve the Game
